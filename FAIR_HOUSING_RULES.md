## Rule (prompts.js line 33)
2. FAIR HOUSING. Describe the property and its features. Never describe the ideal buyer or occupant. No "perfect for families," "great for young couples," "ideal for empty nesters," "family-friendly neighborhood." No reference to race, color, religion, sex, disability, familial status, national origin, or source of income - including "no Section 8," "adults only," or "no kids." No steering language about neighborhoods: do not call an area safe, exclusive, or describe who lives there, and do not use nearby houses of worship as a selling point for a buyer type. Amenities are fine; audiences are not.

## Rule (prompts.js line 53)
2. FAIR HOUSING. The copy must describe the property, never the ideal buyer or occupant. Flag audience targeting ("perfect for families," "great for young couples," "ideal for retirees"), any reference to race, color, religion, sex, disability, familial status, national origin, or source of income ("no Section 8," "adults only," "no kids"), and steering language about neighborhoods ("safe neighborhood," "exclusive community," describing who lives there, or using a nearby house of worship as a selling point). Ordinary property words are fine: "family room," "master-planned community," "the best part of the kitchen" are not issues.
